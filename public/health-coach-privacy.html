Privacy Policy — Personal Coach

Last updated: September 18, 2026

"Personal Coach" is a private, non-commercial application built and operated by Mohammed (moismail.dev) to coordinate fitness and nutrition coaching for a small, fixed group of family members who have personally agreed to participate.

What data this app accesses

With each participant's explicit Google account consent, the app reads and writes fitness and health data via the Google Health API, including: steps, activity minutes, calories, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep sessions, body weight, body fat percentage, workouts, and nutrition logs.

How this data is used

Data is used solely to generate coaching feedback (sleep, recovery, and training recommendations) for the specific individual it belongs to. It is never used for advertising, sold, or shared with any third party.

Where this data is stored

Synced data is stored as plain text logs in a private, access-controlled repository and on a private server operated by the app owner. It is not publicly accessible.

Who can access this data

Only the app owner (acting as coach) and the individual the data belongs to. No other party has access.

Revoking access

Any participant may revoke this app's access at any time via their Google Account permissions page.

Personal Coach is a private family project, not a commercial product or public service.
